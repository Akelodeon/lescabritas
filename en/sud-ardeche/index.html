Your stay at Les Cabritas

Welcome to Les Cabritas, a brand-new house located in Saint-Alban-Auriolles, in the heart of Southern Ardèche, about 10 minutes from Vallon-Pont-d'Arc and 5 minutes from Ruoms and Labeaume.

Ideally located, the house is close to shops (mini-market, bakery, restaurants), rivers and the major tourist sites of the Ardèche Gorges.

You'll be able to enjoy plenty of hiking trails and lovely bike rides straight from the gîte, to explore the region at your own pace.

Discover Southern Ardèche

From Les Cabritas, discover the most beautiful natural and tourist sites of the Ardèche Gorges and its surroundings, grouped by driving time from the gîte.

The Dolmens Trail

A 6 km loop through scrubland, past ancient megaliths.

Mas Daudet

The farm-museum on Alphonse Daudet's Ardèche childhood.

Labeaume

Troglodyte houses, cobbled lanes and hanging gardens on the Beaume.

Bois de Païolive

A forest of karst rocks with astonishing shapes.

Balazuc

One of the most beautiful villages of France, perched above the river.

Ruoms

A road carved into the rock, canoeing and via ferrata.

Pont d'Arc

The iconic natural arch of the Ardèche Gorges.

Grotte de la Cocalière

The ‘diamond cave’, between the Gard and Ardèche.

Les Vans

A well-loved market and the gateway to the Bois de Païolive.

The Chassezac Gorges

A wild canyon famous for its canoe descents.

Largentière

A medieval town built on its silver mines.

Château de Vogüé

A castle above one of the Most Beautiful Villages of France.

Chauvet Cave 2

A fascinating journey into prehistoric art, UNESCO listed.

Ardèche Gorges

The Haute Corniche and its viewpoints over the canyon.

Aiguèze

A perched medieval village, Most Beautiful Villages of France.

Aubenas

Sub-prefecture of the Ardèche and its nine-century château.

The Aven d'Orgnac

Immense galleries and spectacular formations, a Grand Site de France.

Vals-les-Bains

A spa town renowned for its springs and its market.

Grotte de la Madeleine

A concretion cave and a dizzying belvedere over the gorges.

Alba-la-Romaine

Ancient Gallo-Roman capital of the Helvii.

Thines

A hidden Cévennes hamlet with a splendid Romanesque church.

Monts d'Ardèche

Hiking, chestnut groves and volcanoes, a UNESCO Global Geopark.

Saint-Montan

A medieval village saved stone by stone by volunteers.

Thueyts

The Giants' Causeway and its striking basalt organ pipes.

Dolce Via

An outstanding greenway for cycling or walking.

Mont Gerbier de Jonc

The famous volcano where the Loire river rises.

Drôme Valley

Magnificent landscapes and turquoise waters to discover.

Train de l'Ardèche

An authentic ride aboard a historic steam train.

Why choose our Ardèche gîte?

Located in Saint-Alban-Auriolles in the heart of Southern Ardèche, Les Cabritas is a gîte with private pool, ideal for couples, families and friends wishing to discover the Ardèche Gorges in a calm and comfortable setting.

Les Cabritas is also an ideal base for exploring Vallon-Pont-d'Arc, the Pont d'Arc, Ruoms, Labeaume, the Chauvet Cave 2, Balazuc and the most beautiful villages of Southern Ardèche.

Whether you're looking for an Ardèche gîte with a private pool, a family holiday rental, a nature getaway or a relaxing weekend in the heart of the gorges, Les Cabritas welcomes you in a calm and comfortable setting.

The Les Cabritas spirit

Les Cabritas was designed as a place to relax, unwind and reconnect in the heart of Southern Ardèche.

Here, we favour peaceful stays, comfort, respect for the property and authenticity. Whether you're a couple, a family or a group of friends, everything is designed for you to fully enjoy nature, the private pool and the riches of our region.

To preserve the atmosphere our guests and neighbours appreciate, parties and events are not permitted. Pets are not allowed, and we thank you for treating the property as if it were your own.
