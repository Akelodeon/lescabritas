Your stay at Les Cabritas

Welcome to Les Cabritas "Cœur d'Ardèche", a contemporary villa located in Flaviac, at the heart of the Ardèche and Drôme regions.

Just 10 minutes from Privas, 25 minutes from Montélimar and Valence, 30 minutes from the Drôme Valley and around 60 minutes from Pont d'Arc, the house is an ideal base for exploring the region's major tourist sites.

Hiking and cycling enthusiasts will also appreciate the proximity of the ViaRhôna (6 km), the Dolce Via and the Payre greenway.

Discover Ardèche

From Les Cabritas, discover the most beautiful natural and tourist sites of Ardèche.

Pont d'Arc

The iconic natural arch of the Ardèche Gorges.

Chauvet Cave 2

A fascinating immersion into UNESCO-listed prehistoric art.

Balazuc

One of the most beautiful villages in France, perched above the river.

Dolce Via

An outstanding greenway for cycling or walking.

Vals-les-Bains

A spa town renowned for its springs and its market.

Mont Gerbier de Jonc

The famous volcano where the source of the Loire river rises.

Ardèche Steam Train

An authentic ride aboard a historic steam train.

Ruoms and Labeaume

Two villages of southern Ardèche, with ramparts and hanging gardens.

Château de Crussol

A medieval fortress perched above the Rhône valley.

Fort Mahon and the Jaubernie

A hike through the hamlets of Flaviac, starting right from the cottage.

Privas

The county town of Ardèche and its candied chestnut capital, 10 minutes away.

Rochemaure

Medieval village and spectacular Himalayan footbridge over the Rhône.

Aven d'Orgnac

A spectacular cave listed as a Grand Site de France.

The Chassezac

A wild canyon, famous for canoeing amid unspoiled nature.

You can also discover the Drôme

From Les Cabritas, the Drôme isn't far. You can discover perched villages, Provençal markets and wide open natural spaces.

Drôme Valley

Magnificent landscapes and turquoise waters to discover.

Valence

The county town of the Drôme, its Kiosque Peynet and historic centre.

Tour de Crest

The tallest keep in France and 900 years of history.

The Vercors drômois

Sheer cliffs and Resistance history at Vassieux.

The Drôme provençale

Nyons, Buis-les-Baronnies: olive groves and markets with a scent of Provence.

Montélimar

The nougat capital and its Château des Adhémar.

Mirmande

One of the Most Beautiful Villages of France, perched above the Rhône.

Le Palais Idéal du Facteur Cheval

The unique work of a self-taught postman, 33 years of building in Hauterives.

Les Trois Becs

A demanding hike with an exceptional view over the Vercors.

Crocodile Farm

Europe's largest animal park dedicated to reptiles.
