Your stay at Les Cabritas

Welcome to Les Cabritas "Cœur d'Ardèche", a contemporary villa located in Flaviac, at the heart of the Ardèche and Drôme regions.

Just 10 minutes from Privas, 25 minutes from Montélimar and Valence, 30 minutes from the Drôme Valley and around 60 minutes from Pont d'Arc, the house is an ideal base for exploring the region's major tourist sites.

Hiking and cycling enthusiasts will also appreciate the proximity of the ViaRhôna (6 km), the Dolce Via and the Payre greenway.

The house

Beautiful recent 89 m² house set on a 450 m² enclosed plot. A toilet is available on each floor. Private 10 m² pool (4 m x 2.5 m), 1.2 m deep, unheated, secured with an alarm and open from 1 May to 30 September. The pool has no fence or lock: parental supervision of young children is recommended.

Two comfortable bedrooms:

  • 1 bedroom with a 200 x 200 cm king-size bed
  • 1 bedroom with two 90 x 190 cm single beds and a baby cot

100% cotton bed linen, bath towels and welcome kit provided.

Discover Ardèche

From Les Cabritas, discover the most beautiful natural and tourist sites of Ardèche and Drôme.

Pont d'Arc

The iconic natural arch of the Ardèche Gorges.

Chauvet Cave 2

A fascinating immersion into UNESCO-listed prehistoric art.

Balazuc

One of the most beautiful villages in France, perched above the river.

Dolce Via

An outstanding greenway for cycling or walking.

Vals-les-Bains

A spa town renowned for its springs and its market.

Mont Gerbier de Jonc

The famous volcano where the source of the Loire river rises.

Drôme Valley

Magnificent landscapes and turquoise waters to discover.

Ardèche Steam Train

An authentic ride aboard a historic steam train.

Why choose our cottage in Ardèche?

Located in Flaviac at the heart of Ardèche, Les Cabritas is a cottage with a private pool, ideal for couples, families and friends wishing to discover Ardèche in a calm and comfortable setting.

Les Cabritas is also an ideal base for exploring the Ardèche Gorges, Pont d'Arc, Chauvet Cave 2, Balazuc, Vogüé, the ViaRhôna, the Dolce Via, Privas, Aubenas, Montélimar and the Drôme Valley.

Whether you're looking for a cottage in Ardèche with a private pool, a family holiday rental, a nature getaway or a relaxing weekend in the heart of Ardèche, Les Cabritas welcomes you in a calm and comfortable setting.

The Les Cabritas spirit

Les Cabritas was designed as a place to relax, unwind and reconnect at the heart of Ardèche.

Here, we favour peaceful stays, comfort, respect for the property and authenticity. Whether you're a couple, a family or a group of friends, everything is designed for you to fully enjoy nature, the private pool and the treasures of our region.
