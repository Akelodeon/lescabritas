Vals-les-Bains

Nestled in the Volane valley, Vals-les-Bains is a charming spa town known since the 17th century for its mineral waters. Its Belle Époque architecture, spa park planted with rare trees, and famous intermittent spring make it a lovely stop, about 25 to 30 minutes' drive from Les Cabritas in Flaviac, via Privas and Aubenas.

A history tied to its springs

Spa culture in Vals-les-Bains dates back to 1601-1602, when a local fisherman named Pierre Brun is said to have been cured of gallstones after drinking water from one of the local springs. The fame of the 'Belle Fontaine' spring then spread, turning Vals into a renowned spa town, particularly for treating diabetes and digestive disorders. Today, no fewer than 145 different mineral springs have been identified across the commune, a remarkable concentration reflecting the richness of Ardèche's volcanic subsoil.

What to see and do in Vals-les-Bains

The historic centre, nicknamed 'Vieux Vals', is best explored on foot along charming lanes. The spa park, a true three-hectare green haven planted with sequoias, ginkgos and cedars of Lebanon, invites a leisurely stroll. Don't miss the Source Intermittente, a natural geyser that erupts at regular intervals, a rare and spectacular phenomenon to witness. The Maison Champanhet, home to the tourist office and an eco-museum, offers insight into traditional Ardèche crafts and ways of life.

Good to know before you visit

Vals-les-Bains is particularly lovely to explore in the late afternoon, when the heat fades and the Belle Époque architecture is bathed in beautiful light. The town has several cafés and restaurants with terraces, ideal for a break after a day of sightseeing in the region. A lively market is regularly held in the town centre, a great opportunity to discover local produce. Vals-les-Bains also makes a handy base for exploring Aubenas or the surrounding villages, while staying close to Les Cabritas. Spa-goers and wellness enthusiasts can enquire about treatments and activities offered by the thermal establishment, while families will enjoy the many green spaces for a picnic right in the town centre.

Practical info from Les Cabritas

  • Distance from Flaviac: about 25 to 30 min by car
  • Best time to go: all year round
  • Great for: strolling around town, relaxation, spa-goers, families
